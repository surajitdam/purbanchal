Who We Are

Purbanchal Synergies Pvt. Ltd. (Formerly Purbanchal Enterprise), Guwahati, is an electrical services provider and business liaison company. We work with corporates, SMEs, and individuals.

“With our extensive knowledge and 20 years of experience, we have secured a strong position in the electrical market, specializing in turnkey solutions towards HT line and Substation work.”

Our average turnover for the last three years is over 70 crore, consistently increasing year on year. We are the largest and oldest master stockist and channel partner for Larsen & Toubro and Landis+Gyr.
